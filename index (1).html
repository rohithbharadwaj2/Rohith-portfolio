ILC
Online Policy (Behavior Cloning)
  • Decomposes robot observations into ambient light and light-contribution fields for physically consistent multi-intensity synthesis.
  • Distills offline trajectory optimization into a real-time behavioral policy commanding discrete onboard LEDs.
  • Substantially reduces trajectory drift and prevents specular saturation near reflective surfaces.

Framework Pipeline Overview

Stage 1: CLID Decomposition

Separates raw robot feed into ambient component + light field.

Stage 2: Offline OIS Optimization

Calculates ideal lighting schedules balancing image utility vs power.

Stage 3: Real-Time Policy (ILC)

Deploys behavior cloning policy on mobile robot for live adaptive lighting.
