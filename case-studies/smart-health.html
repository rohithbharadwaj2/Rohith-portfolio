System, not notebook.

The archive contains separate patient, doctor and administrator experiences, disease prediction, doctor search, appointment booking, map-oriented doctor views and an ML training/comparison interface.

Portfolio presentation note. The original application dates from an earlier academic project and its interface/implementation reflects that period. This case study intentionally presents the architecture cleanly rather than pretending a redesigned UI was part of the original build.

Responsible framing.

This is an educational/research prototype, not a medical device. Its predictions are not clinically validated and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

The project is associated with the publication Smart Health Prediction System with Data Mining, Nanotechnology Perceptions, Vol. 20, S15 (2024).
